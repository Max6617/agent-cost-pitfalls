INCONCLUSIVE; the interval is the finding and the taxonomy gap is recorded as a defect of the plan.

## 6. GOTCHAS THAT COST TIME TODAY

### 6.1 A multi-edit program aborts at the first failure, and anchors are not as unique as they look

`const scores = rs.map((r) => r.score)` appeared in two functions; the batch died there, silently
skipping the four edits after it. The fix is a longer anchor (`nArms` … `scores`), and checking
`git diff --stat` after a batch.

### 6.2 A module that runs its CLI at import turns a test failure into a load error

When S2/S3 gained no confidence interval, `node --test tools/hard-analysis.test.mjs` reported
`tests 1, fail 1` with a module-level TypeError — because importing the module executed `analyse()`. The
module is now behind an `isMain` guard.

### 6.3 Field names that lie

Checking F35 by hand, `{higher, lower, relPct}` reads as "higher costs more". It does not: `higher` holds
the cheaper arm. Three of four pair directions were inverted before the error was caught against `relPct`
and the cost table. **When two artifacts disagree, check which one is the outlier before correcting the
prose**: the witness's top-level `ties: 47` disagrees with its own `details` (48 non-distinct pairs of 63),
and FINDINGS' table agrees with the details.

### 6.4 A baseline copied from another finding is not a baseline

F50 compared the hard pilot's position distribution against "31/62 (50%)" and "44/66 (67%)" taken from
F45's table. Neither reproduces: every shipped matrix reads **2/62**, and `tools/run-experiment.mjs`'s
header — which was read in this very session — already said the 31/62 belongs to a matrix that "was
destroyed and re-collected". The analysis's own computed numbers were all correct; the error was in a
comparison, which is where a copied figure does its damage. **Derive every number in a claim, including
the ones you are comparing against**, and when two documents disagree, find out which is the outlier
before correcting either. The independent audit caught this in minutes; internal review had already
passed it twice.

### 6.5 `wait_agent` does not cover background subagents

It returns `no-active-peer` for them; the completion arrives as a notification instead. Do not conclude
that an audit has failed because the wait returned immediately.

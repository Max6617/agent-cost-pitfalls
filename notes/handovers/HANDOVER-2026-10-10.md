## 3. The reframe — this is a decision, not a draft note

The paper has lost its cross-harness *mechanism* claim, its third positive classification, and the
robustness of its one confirmatory win. What survives is:

| claim | strength |
|---|---|
| the penalty is **large and model-specific** (haiku +53.84% [+31.25, +76.73], GLM -20.75%, 75-point spread) | strong |
| the **price ratio does not predict it** (three models at ratio 5.00 differ by 52.6 raw points = 70.6% of the spread) | strong, assumption-free in raw form |
| **rankings do not transfer** (median rho 0.238; cross-harness adjacent steps a coin flip) | moderate |
| the **pre-registered replication failed** (R1 0 of 4, R2 fails, stable over 20 seeds) | strong, and now stable |
| **H3** (tool surface costs requests) | weak — a direction, 1.010-9.642 across drops |

**The paper is now an evaluation-critique paper**: careful cross-provider measurement showing that the
summaries everyone uses do not work — the price ratio does not predict the penalty, rankings do not
transfer, our own pre-registered prediction failed, and there is no noise floor to compare against.
That is publishable (measurement / SE venues, evaluation workshops) and NOT a positive-result paper at a
top ML venue. **§8's tiering and the abstract must be rewritten around this.** Do not let the old headline
back in.

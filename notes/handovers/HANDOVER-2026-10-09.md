## 2. Defects found and fixed this session (all test-first, all committed)

1. **The brace guard read a prompt as a template** (`fc712c5`). `multi-file-locale-fallback` is a task
   ABOUT placeholder leaking, so its prompt says "placeholders like {name}"; the launcher scanned
   substituted args for `{...}` and refused to start four harnesses x two seeds on EVERY provider.
   Fixed with `tools/arg-guard.mjs`, which compares each TEMPLATE element with what it produced.
2. **Our own launcher faults were classified as MODEL failures** (`c989b57`). The launch-failure clause
   needs `runId == null`, but launcher errors carry a runId, so they landed in `unexplained` - the one
   bucket the pre-registration says may be read as a model failure - and `--resume` treated them as
   DONE. Fixed with a `launch-failure` text signature in `tools/exclusions.mjs`.
3. **The verify stage counted records, not cells** (`2246201`). A resolved failure still refused the
   sign-off for a complete 800-cell matrix. Fixed with `unresolvedFailures()` in `tools/cell-counts.mjs`.
4. **A completed runner that never exits stalled the pipeline for 2 h 45 min, silently** (`2ac29c9`, and
   `notes/defects/DEFECT-runner-hang-2026-10-07.md`). Both watchdogs stand down once the matrix is complete, so
   nothing watched "complete and never exiting". Fixed with `tools/exit-guard.mjs` - and it fired again
   within hours, costing 15 minutes instead of hours. **The hang is systematic on this machine, not a
   one-off: three occurrences, always a finished runner with no child processes.** Unexplained.
5. **Preflight had no answer to a transiently starved harness** (`tools/retry.mjs` + tests). openhands
   made zero requests while a second collector ran, and the matrix refused to start with no way to wait
   it out. 3 attempts, 45 s apart, each into its own probe directory.
6. **A test asserted a snapshot instead of a property** (`tools/dataset-selection.test.mjs`): "every
   tasks-b dataset is reported missing" went red the moment the first matrix landed.

Plus the incident record: `notes/defects/INCIDENT-haiku-account-limit-2026-10-09.md`.

### Three traps, all paid for

1. **NEVER kill a terminal window.** On Windows 11, console apps launched by Explorer are handed off to
   Windows Terminal, so a "plain, idle" terminal was hosting the DSH app's own console - `taskkill /T`
   on it killed the session AND the collectors, mid-command. Parent-process checks do not reveal console
   host attachment. Closing windows is the human's job; the agent's job is to say which is safe.
2. **Orphaned gateways accumulate after every interrupted run** and cost a collector its start-up (a new
   collector picked a port held by a dead gateway enforcing the WRONG MODEL and correctly refused).
   Cleared by hand four times. Sweep: kill `measure-gateway` processes older than 20 minutes.
3. **Memory is the binding constraint, not CPU.** 15.7 GB total; two collectors (8 concurrent agents)
   drove it to 0.5 GB free and made the laptop unusable. One collector at a time is the tested load.
   WSL's `vmmemWSL` was holding ~0.6 GB for two idle distros; stopped, and capped at 6 GB in
   `~/.wslconfig`. WSL/Docker are NOT used by the study (openhands headless does not use Docker).

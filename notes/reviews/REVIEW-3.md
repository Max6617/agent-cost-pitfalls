## 1. Verdict, in five sentences

1. The F44 pass is better than its predecessors and still fails its own standard: every corrected *headline aggregate* reproduces from primary data, but the pass introduced at least three new defects in the exact classes it exists to fix — the worst being an exclusion-rule "fix" that cannot fire, because the four phrasings F44 §2 says were "added" were added to `SIGNATURES` and never to `INFRASTRUCTURE`, so **41 genuine provider-fault runs — including one inside a published panel matrix — are still analysed as model failures, now relabelled "unexplained", the very bucket the rule defines as the only one readable as a model failure**.
2. The pass's new statistical machinery has the same property: A2b's "3 of 7 survive Holm" is a Monte-Carlo artefact (**2 of 7**; Qwen's exact sign-flip p = 0.0104 fails its 0.0100 threshold — verified by the lead with two independent 2×10⁶-draw computations), its seven p-values depend on contrast order through one shared RNG stream — the defect F44 §1 fixed fifty lines above — and under the family PRE-REGISTRATION §6's text actually mandates, **F44's own flagship statistic (the Haiku McNemar, p = 0.0072) fails the Holm correction**.
3. The new safety tool inverts both of its claims — `preflight.mjs` passes a harness whose every request failed whenever the gateway logs `status: 0` (`0 < 400`), and any runner exception fails a working preflight via a phantom `undefined` harness row — and the new Sonnet A/B prices every one of its 216 runs on the **Haiku** card, so every Sonnet dollar figure in F37 is exactly half the true value.
4. The register was swept incompletely for the third time: F38's table never moved though F44 §2 says it did; F35 now contradicts itself (47 vs 48) because F44 updated the headline and not the table; F34 and F31 carry pre-correction numbers including a **sign flip on luna's PTC differential** (+4.2% printed, −0.2% actual); 22 printed intervals moved though F44 §1 says none did; and notes/reviews/REVIEW-RESPONSE.md still teaches the retracted placebo verdict verbatim.
5. The paper's claim survives anyway — the noise floor, the rank-transfer statistics, the caching mechanism, and every H-label reproduce under hostile recomputation, and the corrections' *direction* is right almost everywhere — but the record on clean correction passes is now 0-for-3, and the defect has moved one layer deeper again: from the reading of results, to the tools, to **the correction pass's claims about its own fixes**.

(What checks out: the runner has no exit-nonzero path for failed *requests*, verified by full source read; the runId join is collision-safe; the probe-task and `--only` selection are sound on current task dirs.)

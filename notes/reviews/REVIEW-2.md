## 2. The five worst things, ranked

### #1 — F29's "one interval excludes zero" is an artifact of a broken RNG; the RNG also biases the experiment's arm shuffle

- `notes/records/FINDINGS.md:976`: *"Two arms with no treatment difference at all differ by up to 7.7%, and one interval excludes zero."* The CI `[0.1%, 16.6%]` (archived `analysis/panel-analysis.json` A7 `excludesZero: true`) is produced by `clusterBootstrap` (`tools/panel-analysis.mjs:209-221`): an LCG evaluated in doubles.
- **The LCG is catastrophically broken, proved.** `(s*1103515245 + 12345) & 0x7fffffff` in doubles: the product exceeds 2^53 on the *first* step for two of the three seeds in use, and on every subsequent step for all three. 100,000/100,000 states diverge from the BigInt-exact stream; the corrupted stream falls into a **cycle of length 10,466** (all three seeds, Brent's algorithm), yields only ~15,820 distinct values per million draws, and fails uniformity at **z > 2,000** (χ², serial, 2D). The exact same LCG in BigInt passes every test — the constants are fine; the double-precision evaluation is not.
- **Material consequence:** with BigInt-exact, xorshift128+, mulberry32, and splitmix32 streams, and at 1e6 converged iterations, the placebo CI-1 is ≈ **[−0.33%, +16.45%]** — *includes* zero. Corroborated by paired t (t = 1.77, p = 0.077), Wilcoxon (p = 0.142), and sign-flip permutation (p = 0.089). Over 40 independent seeds the lower endpoint ranged [−0.604%, −0.002%]: **0 of 40 positive**. The published "+0.1%" exists only for one LCG seed at 5,000 draws.
- **The same defect biases the launcher.** `tools/run-experiment.mjs:88-106` shuffles arm order with the same double-LCG and consumes its *low* bits (`s % (i+1)`), which the rounding zeroes (states are multiples of 256/512). Measured: `dsh:native` occupies position 0 in **0/62** ds-full runs, `dsh:ptc` in **31/62**. Position fixed-effects absorb ~34% of the raw +7.7% placebo diff. This bias is inherited by every matrix this runner launched, not just ds-full.
- **"Byte-identical in treatment" is also false.** F29 (`notes/records/FINDINGS.md:968`) and notes/reviews/REVIEW-RESPONSE.md:161 assert byte-identity; measured, the two arms' first requests differ by exactly +30 bytes on all 62 (task,seed) pairs = 6 copies of the arm-id string, and the dsh system prompt's final line names the arm via the workdir path (verified in archived session logs). Mitigating: the leak primes in the *opposite* direction to the observed effect, and the effect is statistically zero anyway.
- What survives: the +7.7%/+3.6% point estimates (honest summaries of run-to-run variance, mostly output-length noise) and the *practical* gauge "below ~8% is not measurable at n=62/arm" (honest CI half-width ≈ 8.4pp). What must be retracted: "and one interval excludes zero" and `excludesZero: true`.
